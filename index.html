The brain

Claude

⇄ Swappable

The intelligence sits behind a model seam. Because the skills, routines, data and connectors are all separate from the model, the brain can be swapped for another AI without rebuilding the machine. The same is true one layer down: the back end is a part you can change too. You are never locked to one vendor.

Why it is shaped like this: four simple tables that point at each other. Ask the assistant what the last thing said to someone was and it walks the links: finds the person, finds their company, finds the deals attached, finds every interaction logged against them. That is the whole trick. Everything the assistant learns lands here, structured and linked, so the next question is easy to answer. Whether that lives in Notion or in HubSpot for Authi is exactly the sort of thing we would work out together rather than guess at now.

It all lives in one repo

The whole assistant is one version-controlled repository. The instructions, every skill, every routine, all as readable files. Nothing hidden in a black box, which matters when your CTO wants to look under the bonnet.

Every change is tracked, reviewable and reversible. Deploy is one step: merge the change and it is live for the team. merge = deploy

Everything runs in your own accounts, not ours. It is an asset you own, not a subscription you rent.
