⇄ Swappable

The intelligence sits behind a model seam. Because the skills, routines, data and connectors are all separate from the model, the brain can be swapped for another AI without rebuilding the machine. The same is true one layer down: HubSpot, Notion and every connector sit behind their own seam, so a tool can be changed without touching the rest. The model is a part you can swap, not the whole machine, and you are never locked to one vendor.

Campaign logevery campaign, its goal, where it ran, what came back, and the wash-up, so the next pitch cites the last result
Channel + referralsevery referral that comes in through Verifone or EFTPOS NZ, who sent it, and what happened to it, because a channel lead that goes quiet is nobody's job by default
Interactionsevery meeting, call and key thread, filed to the account and the HubSpot deal
Objection libraryevery objection heard, which room it came from, and the answer that actually worked
The playbookthe Connect, Crowd and merchant pitches, their NZ, AU and US variants, and the standards every seat writes to
How it links: HubSpot holds the deal, the brain layer holds the story. Every record in Notion carries the HubSpot ID of the company or deal it belongs to, so the two can never disagree about who is who, and one merchant who is also a Connect customer stays one company with two threads rather than two half-records. When the brain needs to change a deal it writes straight into HubSpot over its own supported connection, in your name and with your permissions, rather than editing a copy and syncing later. Nothing is stored twice, so there is never a second CRM to keep in step, and never a question of which version is true.

It all lives in one repo

The whole assistant is one version-controlled repository. The instructions, every skill, every routine, all as readable files. Nothing hidden in a black box, which matters when your CTO wants to look under the bonnet.

Every change is tracked, reviewable and reversible. Deploy is one step: merge the change and it is live for the team. merge = deploy

Everything runs in Authi's own accounts, not ours. If we disappeared the day after we finished, it would keep running. It is an asset you own, not a subscription you rent.
